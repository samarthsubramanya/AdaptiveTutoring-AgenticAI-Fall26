# Check an expanded expression

Call verify_symbolic_answer with operation="expand". Preserve the original parentheses and require expanded form. Equivalent factored form is not necessarily the requested answer.

Never execute student text. Confirm ambiguous word-problem interpretations first. A correct final answer alone does not certify mastery.
